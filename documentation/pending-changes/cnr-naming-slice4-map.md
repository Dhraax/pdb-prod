# CNR naming normalisation — slice 4 map

The exact rename applied by commit `74bc88cd`: every old resref, the tag it
carried, and its new resref and tag. This table, not git's rename detection, is
the authoritative record. Git pairs renames by content similarity, and the three
price copies of the light hammer were byte-identical apart from their resref, so
`git diff -M` pairs them differently depending on its options.

Tag equals resref except in the owner-approved shared-tool families, marked **shared**.

# Oficios changelog — August 2026

## 2026-08-28 — Carpentry planks stack and test stock stays clean

**What changed.** The eight sawmill recipes now give their products the exact
names used by the corresponding `src/cnr/uti/carptablon_*` blueprints and by
the carpenter's material chest. This includes lowercase wood names such as
`Tablones de abeto`; their tags and resrefs already matched. A crafted plank
and its chest copy can therefore join the same stack. The custom item palette
also now labels `carplenyo_olmo`, `carptablon_cipre`, and `carptablon_olmo` with
the same Cedro and Leñocaso names used by their blueprints.

The six infinite DEV material chests no longer copy arbitrary local variables
when replenishing stock. They restore only `CNR_STOCK`, `CNR_OFICIO`, and
`CNR_LOOT_TIER`, and `CNR_STOCK` items are exempt from the same-account
character-transfer guard. A CD-key ownership marker can no longer become part
of permanent chest stock or make materials disappear during multi-character
testing. Normal non-test items remain protected.

**Test.** Craft recipe 2003, *Tablones de abeto*, and take
`carptablon_abeto` from the carpenter chest; the two items must stack. Repeat
for all eight sawmill products, and confirm the builder palette shows the same
names. With two characters on the same CD key, remove and exchange marked
materials from each of the six infinite chests; no item must be destroyed and
every marked removal must replenish the chest. Confirm that the arcane samples
retain `CNR_OFICIO`, the extractor samples retain `CNR_LOOT_TIER`, and a normal
item without `CNR_STOCK` is still rejected by the same-account transfer guard.

**Still owed.** Apply the regenerated catalogue with `db-apply.sh`, then build,
deploy, and run the in-game test above. Planks already crafted with an old
custom-capitalized name are not renamed automatically.

**Commits.** This change.

## 2026-08-27 — Arcane spell slots offer every spell level

**What changed.** Every *Huecos de conjuro* property at the arcane table now
offers all spell levels in that class progression. Full progressions offer
levels 1 through 6 for one through six essences. Progressions capped at level 3
offer levels 1 through 3 for two through four essences. The top costs remain the
reviewed six and four essences.

The chosen level is a real catalogue step with its own DC and XP, and the
server-side applier validates that exact step again. This adds 42 spell-slot
steps to the generated arcane data, from 442 to 484; it does not require a new
window or applier path.

**Test.** At Arcane level 20, open *Huecos de conjuro* for each of the twelve
classes. Cleric, bard, wizard, druid, sorcerer and favored soul must list levels
1–6. Ranger, paladin, dark paladin, avenging paladin, knight of the light and
artificer must list levels 1–3. Enchant two full-progression items, one at level
1 and one at level 6, and two truncated-progression items, one at level 1 and
one at level 3. Confirm each item property names the selected class and level,
the displayed DC matches the roll message, and the table consumes respectively
1/6 and 2/4 essences.

**Still owed.** Apply the regenerated catalogue with `db-apply.sh`, then build,
deploy and run the in-game test above. Existing enchanted items do not change.

**Commits.** This change.

## 2026-08-27 — The sorcerer's dagger can be forged, and enchanted

A caster no longer has to carry a staff to have something the arcane table will
work on. The **sorcerer's dagger** is now the fiftieth product of the smith's
weapon group, so any of the fifteen metal recipes at the anvil offers it, and the
arcane table accepts it for everything it already accepted a mage staff for: the
76 properties of the *equipo* group — skills, saving throws, **spell slots**,
damage immunity, abilities.

The copper practice recipe forges it clean, with no properties at all, which is
the closest thing to the carpenter's plain oak staff. Any real metal gives it
that metal's two weapon properties, and there is room under the eight-property
ceiling for what the enchanter adds.

This reverses the decision of 2026-08-17 that the dagger stayed out of the
trades. Nothing about the item changed; what changed is that the arcane table
only admits what a trade has made, so while the dagger was loot only, no mage
could ever enchant one.

The design now names the three things it used to call "baston": the carpenter's
quarterstaff, the mage staff and the mage dagger, in the CSV, in `arcano.json`
and in the generator, so the label and the base-item table cannot drift apart
again.

**Test.**

1. At the anvil, craft *Daga de hechicería* in copper and in one real metal.
   The copper one must come out with no properties; the metal one with two.
2. Put the copper one on the arcane table. It must be accepted — before this it
   answered *"Pon en la mesa un solo objeto de oficio sin encantar"* for any
   dagger, because loot carries no trade mark.
3. Enchant it with **Huecos de conjuro** of your class, and with a skill. Both
   must appear in the item's property list, not only in the name.
4. A dagger that dropped as loot (`item_dagahechi`) is **still** not enchantable,
   and that is intended: the table only takes what a trade made.

**Still owed.**

- A regenerated database: `db-apply.sh` has not been run.
- The dagger is not in a hak; the blueprint ships in the module.
- Boots, gloves, bracers, cloaks and the leather belt — fifty crafted wearables —
  still cannot be enchanted at all. The design does not name them in `equipo`
  either, so it needs a decision, not a patch. See `arcane-plan.md`.

**Commits.** pendiente

## 2026-08-27 — Arcane stops offering what the engine refuses, and stops lying about it

Three reports from the same table, one cause each.

**The nunchaku, and seventeen more.** Arcane decided which weapons it accepted
from four lists written by hand in `build_arcane.py`, copied from NWN vanilla,
ending at base item row 111. Everything the hak added above that was invisible to
it — including eighteen weapons the trades themselves forge: nunchaku, sai,
katar, falchion, both picks, maul, heavy mace, short spear, one-handed trident,
assassin's dagger, double scimitar, wind-fire wheel, maug sword, the three huge
weapons and the longspear. The smith sold you a maul and the table answered *"Ese
objeto no admite esta propiedad."*

The lists are gone. The weapon groups are now read at every build from what the
catalogue actually forges, sorted by the `PropColumn` of `baseitems.2da`. A new
weapon at the anvil is enchantable the day it ships, and nothing that is not
crafted ever appears — a weapon with no trade mark could never reach the table
anyway. **The sling leaves the ranged group** for that reason: nobody forges one.

**The bow and the on-hit effect.** `itemprops.2da` row 48 forbids On Hit on every
launcher — bow, crossbow, sling. The table offered all ten anyway, the engine
dropped the property without a word, and the arrow was left named and marked. On
hit now goes where it works: **melee, thrown weapons and ammunition**. An archer
enchants the arrows, the bolts and the bullets, which is what delivers the hit.
Keen and Massive Criticals moved the opposite way — legal on a launcher, refused
on ammunition — so they no longer appear on arrows.

Every property was cross-checked against the `PropColumn` of every base item in
its group afterwards: **zero pairs the menu offers and the engine would refuse.**

**And the item that came back [Encantado] with nothing on it.** `CnrProp_Apply`
returned nothing, so the table could not tell a property that landed from one the
engine had dropped. It renamed, signed, marked and charged regardless — and the
mark is what makes an enchantment irreversible, so that item was finished
forever. It now answers whether the item really gained a property, measured on
the item itself because `AddItemProperty` is void and refuses in silence. When it
did not: no name, no description, no mark, not identified, the item exactly as it
went in and available for another attempt. The material is still spent, the same
way a failed roll spends it.

The design now names the three different things it used to call *"Todas las
armas"*, in the CSV, in `arcano.json` and in the generator.

**Test.**

1. **Nunchaku.** Forge one at the anvil, put it on the table, apply *Daño
   contundente*. It must be accepted and the damage must appear in the item's
   property list. Same with a maul, a katar and a huge greatsword.
2. **Bow.** *Efecto al golpear* must no longer be offered on a bow, a crossbow or
   a sling at all.
3. **Arrows.** It must be offered on a crafted arrow, bolt and bullet, and the
   property must appear on them.
4. **Arrows again.** *Afiladura* and *Críticos masivos* must **not** be offered
   on ammunition any more. They stay on melee weapons and on bows.
5. **The floor.** There should now be no way to reach it, which is the point. If
   any enchantment ever fails, the item must come back with its original name and
   no `[Encantado]`, and the log carries `[CNR] Arcane property … did not apply`.

**Still owed.**

- A regenerated database: `db-apply.sh` has not been run.
- The report of *Vellosidad de alaghi · contundente · 1d8* applying nothing is
  not confirmed closed. The data was correct and the property is legal on every
  weapon, so the leading explanation is that the weapon was one of the eighteen
  and the table refused it — which this fixes. If it happens again it will now
  say so in the log instead of looking like a success.
- Boots, gloves, bracers, cloaks and the leather belt — fifty crafted wearables —
  still cannot be enchanted. The design does not name them in `equipo`; it needs
  a decision.
- `cnr_i_craft` calls `CnrProp_Apply` too and still ignores the answer. A recipe
  property that the engine refuses is silent there the same way it used to be
  here.

**Commits.** pendiente

## 2026-08-27 — The mithril helmet stops being an arandur helmet

A mithril helmet gave 4 or 5 AC and **10% slashing resistance**. So did an
arandur one. Arandur is tier 3 and opens at level 16; mithril is tier 4, opens at
19, and needs a rarer ingot and a rarer metal for the identical result. Reported
from play.

**The mithril helmet now gives 15%.** Both of them — the AC5 one and the light
AC4 one. The ladder reads arandur 10, mithril 15, adamantita 20.

Where the 10% came from: the design asks the mithril helmet for -20% arcane
spell failure, and `itemprops.2da` leaves the helmet column empty on that
property, so the engine dropped it in silence. That was found on 2026-08-23 and
slashing resistance was put in its place — copied, as it turns out, from the
arandur row. Nothing else about mithril changed: the armour and the shields keep
their spell-failure reduction, which their own column does allow.

**Test.** Forge a *Yelmo de mithril* and a *Yelmo ligero de mithril*. Both must
read 15% slashing resistance, and their AC must stay at 5 and 4. An arandur
helmet must still read 10%.

**Still owed.** A regenerated database: `db-apply.sh` has not been run. Helmets
already forged keep the 10% they were made with; only new ones get 15%.

**Commits.** pendiente
